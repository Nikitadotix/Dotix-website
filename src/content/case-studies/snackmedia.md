## Project Overview

SnackMedia is an influencer marketing agency working with creators and brands worldwide. Their previous online presence didn’t reflect the scale of their services or the quality of their work. They needed a website that was fast, modern, professional, and optimized for search visibility.

Dotix designed and developed a **complete multi-page website** that aligns with SnackMedia’s brand identity, communicates their offerings clearly, and positions them as a trusted partner for brands and influencers.

---

## What We Created

The project included a fully structured multi-page website covering home, services, about, contact, and additional supporting pages required for the agency’s operations. Each page was designed with modern UI patterns and smooth, meaningful animations to enhance user experience while maintaining clarity.

A built-in **contact form** allows the agency to receive inquiries directly, helping streamline communication with potential clients and influencers.

Dotix also handled the end-to-end **deployment**, ensuring the site runs efficiently in production with strong caching, accessibility, and indexing performance.

---

## Tech Stack Used (And Why)

### Next.js, Tailwind CSS, Motion (formerly Framer Motion)

The website was developed using **Next.js**, chosen for its speed, routing flexibility, and SEO advantages. With server-side rendering and optimized builds, the site loads fast and ranks well.

**Tailwind CSS** enabled a clean, scalable design system that ensured consistency across all pages.

For animations, we used **Motion**, providing smooth transitions and subtle effects that enhance the brand experience without compromising performance.

This combination ensures reliability, modern aesthetics, and high search engine visibility.

---

## Impact & Results

The website gave SnackMedia a stronger digital presence and a modern brand image. Because of the improved SEO structure and lightweight performance, the agency experienced higher visibility and increased organic traffic.

A professional website builds confidence—SnackMedia now **closes deals faster** and presents itself more effectively to potential collaborators and brands.

---

## Conclusion

Dotix delivered a multi-page website that reflects the energy and professionalism of SnackMedia. With high-performance pages, polished UI, and excellent search optimization, the site establishes a strong foundation for the agency’s continued growth.
