## Project Overview

**Buzzr** is a complete digital system designed for [SnackMedia](https://snackmedia.io?ref=dotix.io), a fast-growing influencer marketing agency. The platform solves major industry challenges such as slow collaboration cycles, manual campaign tracking, scattered communication, and the difficulty of managing influencer relationships at scale.

Dotix engineered Buzzr as a unified ecosystem where brands, influencers, and agency teams can collaborate smoothly, track progress in real time, and maintain full visibility across campaigns.

---

## What We Created

The solution included a cross-platform mobile app, an advanced admin dashboard, a high-performance marketing website, and full UI/UX design.

The **mobile application** allows influencers to view campaigns, accept or decline collaborations, submit deliverables, track approval status, and receive instant updates. The experience is built to be fast, intuitive, and optimized for active creators.

The **admin dashboard** gives SnackMedia complete control over campaign operations. Their team can create and manage campaigns, assign influencers, review submitted content, monitor analytics, and handle day-to-day operations without friction.

The **marketing website** serves as the product’s public presence, clearly communicating features and benefits while driving lead conversions with fast load times and SEO-friendly performance.

All **UI/UX screens** were designed in Figma, ensuring visual clarity, smooth navigation, and a consistent experience across the entire product ecosystem.

---

## Tech Stack Used (And Why It Was Ideal)

### Next.js, Tailwind CSS, shadcn, Firebase Notifications (Dashboard)

The dashboard was built using **Next.js** for server-side rendering and speed, **Tailwind CSS** and **shadcn** for clean, efficient styling, and **Firebase** for real-time notifications. This combination ensures high performance, security, and smooth handling of complex workflows.

### Expo, React Native, TypeScript, Tailwind (Mobile App)

The mobile app was developed with **Expo** and **React Native**, allowing rapid development and a shared codebase for iOS and Android. **TypeScript** increases reliability, while Tailwind ensures consistent styling. With **Expo Notifications**, influencers receive timely updates directly on their devices.

### Node.js, Express, TypeScript, MySQL (Backend)

The backend uses **Node.js** and **Express** for scalable API architecture, **TypeScript** for safety, and **MySQL** as a robust relational database. This stack provides stability, speed, and smooth integration across both web and mobile clients.

### Static HTML, Tailwind CSS (Marketing Website)

The website was developed with **Static HTML** to achieve maximum SEO performance and fast indexing. Combined with **Tailwind CSS**, it delivers excellent loading speeds and exceptional Lighthouse scores.

### Figma (Design)

All product interfaces were designed in Figma, ensuring clarity, brand alignment, and a visually seamless experience.
