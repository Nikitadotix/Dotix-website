## Project Overview

Inloop Media, a digital marketing agency focused on helping brands grow online, needed a modern, fast, and trustworthy website that reflects its capabilities. The previous site lacked visual impact and speed—two essential factors for agencies pitching high-value clients.

Dotix designed and developed a **fully modern, animated, SEO-optimized landing website** for Inloop Media to strengthen online presence, build trust, and improve conversions.

---

## What We Created

Dotix delivered a complete front-end experience tailored to a digital-first brand. The website features clean layout structures, smooth animations, and a polished modern UI to reflect the agency’s professionalism. Every section was designed to communicate credibility and help visitors understand Inloop Media’s services at a glance.

A fully functional **contact form** was integrated to streamline lead capture and improve communication flow with potential clients. The entire site was optimized for speed, clarity, and SEO.

After development, Dotix handled the **full deployment**, ensuring smooth hosting, performance optimization, and a production-ready setup.

---

## Tech Stack Used (And Why)

### Astro, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS

The website was built with **Astro**, paired with **TypeScript** and **Tailwind CSS**. Astro outputs pure **static HTML**, which is one of the strongest foundations for SEO and page-load performance. This ensures that search engines can crawl the site easily and rank it higher.

Tailwind CSS provided rapid styling and design consistency, while TypeScript added structure and reliability.

This stack results in extremely fast loading, lightweight pages, and excellent Lighthouse scores—critical advantages for agencies competing in the digital marketing space.

The new website immediately improved Inloop Media’s digital presence. With a cleaner interface, stronger branding, and faster page load times, the agency experienced increased reach and higher engagement.

A well-designed website builds trust, and as a result, Inloop Media is now able to **close deals faster**, make a stronger first impression, and present itself as a modern, capable marketing partner.
