## 2. What are Cookies, Pixels and Local Storage?

Cookies are small files that websites place on your computer while you browse the internet. Like many commercial websites, we employ cookies to enhance your experience. These cookies and similar technologies serve various purposes, such as facilitating efficient navigation between pages, remembering your preferences, and generally improving user experience. Additionally, cookies and other technologies may help us measure the effectiveness of our marketing efforts and make your use of the Platform and its features more relevant and beneficial.

Pixel tags, also known as web beacons or pixels, are tiny pieces of code embedded in a web page or an email notification. They enable companies to gather information such as your IP address, the time you viewed the pixel, and the type of browser you used. We utilize pixel tags to determine if you’ve interacted with content on our Platform, which aids us in measuring and enhancing our Platform and personalizing your experience.

Local storage allows websites to store information directly on your computer or mobile device. It is primarily used for storing and retrieving data in HTML pages from the same domain. We use local storage to tailor the content we present to you based on your past interactions with our Platform.

It’s important to note that cookies (along with the technologies mentioned above) collect both personal information and non-identifiable information.

## 3. How and Why do We Use Cookies?

We utilize both first-party cookies (set by us) and third-party cookies (set by servers outside our Site's domain). Some of these cookies or similar technologies are classified as "strictly necessary," meaning they are essential for the Site's operation. Without these cookies, the Site would not function properly. Other cookies or similar technologies, while not essential, help us enhance our Platform and measure user engagement. Below is a detailed explanation of why we use cookies:

**Strictly Necessary or Essential Cookies:** These cookies are crucial for the Site's functionality and cannot be disabled in our systems. For instance, we use authentication cookies to identify you during your browsing session after you log in to our websites. We must use these essential cookies to fulfill our legitimate interests in providing you with the core functionality of the Site.

**Functionality Cookies:** These cookies enable additional features on our Site, such as remembering your preferences (e.g., username) and assisting us in delivering support or payment services. This functionality enhances the user experience and allows us to provide better services and a more efficient Platform.

**Performance and Analytics Cookies:** These cookies help us track visits and traffic sources, enabling us to measure and improve our Site's performance. They provide insights into which pages are most and least popular and how visitors navigate through the Site. Performance cookies support our analytics efforts by compiling statistics and data regarding your interactions with the Site, including details about how and when you access it, the frequency of your visits, your actions on the Site, and other relevant traffic and usage trends.
